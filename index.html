The IronMind Method

High performers don't need motivation. They need a game plan.

A structured engagement built around your situation, run over five phases. Most clients commit to a 90-day arc, then continue quarterly.

01
Deep Dive & Diagnostic

The Iron Audit

First we map out exactly where you are. We dissect your life: career, habits, health, relationships. With brutal honesty. What are the sticking points? Where are you leaking energy or control? This is where we lay all the cards on the table, confidentially and without judgment. Many clients say this initial session alone is more candid than years of surface-level therapy.

02
Custom Game Plan

The Forge Plan

Next, I design a coaching plan that is 100% custom to you. No generic advice. We name the patterns that are costing you most, the habits that need to break, and the new ones that need to take their place. Weekly goals. Scripts for the conversations you've been avoiding. A clear runway for the next ninety days.

03
1:1 Coaching Sessions

Weekly clarity in the trenches

We meet on secure video or phone once a week for 60 minutes. We tackle the most pressing challenge on the table. Had a hard week? We break it down and pull the lesson out of it. Big decision coming? We rehearse it. Each session converts insight into action. You leave with a next move you can actually take.

04
Real-Time Accountability

Support between sessions

Change doesn't only happen on Tuesdays at 4pm. That's why I'm in your corner between sessions too. Text quick support? Voice memo before a difficult meeting? You have it. The next big audit conversation, the post-mortem on what just happened. They all get a place to land in real time.

05
Iterate, Celebrate, Sharpen

The compound effect, in 90 days

As you progress, we refine the plan. And we mark the wins. First thirty days sober. Regained the trust of your spouse. Closed the quarter without burning out. These deserve being named out loud. By ninety days, what we built becomes how you operate. Most clients continue with a quarterly tune-up or an annual intensive to keep the edge sharp.

Questions

Frequently asked.

How private is this, really?

Sessions are on a secure video or phone line. Nothing about working together is published, posted, or referenced anywhere. Your name does not appear on the testimonials wall unless you put it there. The relationship is structured the way attorneys structure theirs. Privileged by design.

What does a typical engagement look like?

A 90-day initial arc, weekly 60-minute sessions, with text access between sessions for when something needs immediate eyes. Many clients continue into quarterly tune-ups or an annual intensive rhythm after the first ninety days.

What does it cost?

By application. The investment is shared after the Iron Audit conversation so we both know what is actually on the table. Premium positioning, not luxury pricing. We work with leaders, not estates.

I'm in recovery. Is this for me?

Yes. Addiction recovery and grief work are core to what Kenny coaches. He's lived through it and has the clinical training (Master's in Addiction Counseling, in progress) to back the lived experience. This is not therapy, and it's not a substitute for clinical care. It runs alongside it.

What if I relapse during the engagement?

Relapse doesn't end the engagement. It informs it. The work was always going to include the days you don't want to show up. Those are the days we work hardest.

What if I'm not in crisis, just feel something has shifted?

The best time to do this work is before crisis. If you can feel something erode, that's a signal worth following. The Identity Score™ For Executives is built to map exactly where.
