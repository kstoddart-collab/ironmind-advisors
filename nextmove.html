The big idea

Somewhere around 50, the questions begin to change.

For decades, the questions were predictable.

  • Where will we work?
  • How much should we save?
  • Where will we raise our family?
  • How do we pay off the house?
  • How much do we need for retirement?

What are we building toward now?

  • Do we still want to live here?
  • How much money is actually enough?
  • When does saving become living?
  • What happens if one of us keeps working and the other doesn't?
  • Who are we without our careers?

If we already have enough to begin living differently, what exactly are we waiting for?

The Next Move™ isn't a retirement calculator. It's a clarity assessment for couples approaching their next chapter — to help two people work out not simply whether they can make a change, but whether they understand the life they want to create together.

How it works

Two people. Sixteen statements. One honest comparison.

  1. Take it separately

    Each partner answers alone, based on what they believe today. That matters — one partner shouldn't shape the other's answers.

  2. Sixteen statements

    Rate each from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). It takes about five minutes and you get your Next Move Score right away.

  3. Compare the gaps

    Send your partner your private link. When they finish, you both see where you're aligned, where you differ, and which conversation comes first.
