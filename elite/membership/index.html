05 — Fit

Who this is for

Membership is small on purpose. It works for some people and not others.

This works if you're

  • Ambitious. You want more than what's been handed to you, and you're willing to say so out loud.
  • Coachable. You can take hard feedback without needing a week to recover from it.
  • Quietly struggling. You look fine from the outside. Privately it's imposter syndrome, anxiety, and the early edge of burnout — at 23.

This doesn't work if

  • You're here because somebody else wants you to be, and you've already decided it's a waste of a Friday.
  • You want tactics without the identity work underneath them.
  • You want a cheerleader. Kenny will be in your corner and he will still tell you the truth.
