At your service. Not on a schedule.

Access

  • Same-day emergency sessions. A board revolt, a termination, a diagnosis, a resignation you didn’t see coming. I clear the calendar.
  • Weekly 60-minute working session. The IronMind Method — diagnostic, plan, execution, recalibration — run for a full year, not a weekend.
  • Monthly half-day deep dive. In person in Jacksonville Beach, at your office, or wherever you are. Travel included within the continental U.S.

Measurement
  • Identity Gain OS™ baseline and quarterly re-score. Your standing across all eight domains, so the work is tracked against a number and not a feeling.

  • Confidential 360. Structured interviews with the people who see you most — spouse, chief of staff, board chair — reported only to you.
  • Written executive report, twice a year. Where you were, where you are, what I see coming. Eyes only.
Scope
  • The decisions that don’t fit on an agenda. Succession, exit, a hostile board, a co-founder who has to go, the offer you shouldn’t take.

  • The Superman cape, off. The private work: burnout, identity, the gap between the person in the meeting and the person who drives home. Backed by clinical mental health training, delivered by someone who has lived it.
