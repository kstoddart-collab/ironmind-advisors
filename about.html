Meet the founder

Kenny Stoddart.

Executive leader. Endurance athlete. Cancer survivor. Coach. A person still doing the work of becoming.

Kenny founded IronMind Advisors to bring his experience in leadership, performance, and personal rebuilding into a mission centered on people.

He believes that confidence, resilience, and a clear sense of identity can be developed — and that the person underneath the performance deserves as much attention as the result.

Experience, perspective, purpose

Beyond the title. Beyond the finish line.

For more than 27 years, Kenny lived in executive environments where results mattered and expectations were high. He built a career in cybersecurity leadership and competed in endurance sports, learning the discipline of preparation, consistent effort, and responding when the plan changes.

But achievement did not answer every question. Burnout, recovery, and cancer forced him to look beyond what he accomplished and examine who he was underneath it.

Rebuilding gave him a different understanding of strength: asking for help, accepting honest feedback, reconnecting with values, and taking responsibility for the next step. That experience continues to shape his work.

His aim is to help others build that foundation earlier — whether they are preparing for college, starting a career, carrying leadership responsibility, or navigating a transition.
